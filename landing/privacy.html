2. How we use data

3. Storage

Session data may be stored locally in your browser (for example localStorage) and on our servers when a backend is connected. We retain data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information with processors that help run the Service (hosting, analytics, anti-fraud), or when required by law.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use essential cookies/local storage for sign-in state and preferences. You can clear them in your browser; some features may stop working until you sign in again.

6. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data. Contact support@garbona.local.

7. Children

The Service is for users 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
